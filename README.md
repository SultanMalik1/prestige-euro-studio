# Prestige Auto Polish

Role & Goal:

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and web developer. Create a modern, stylish, highly polished, and fully responsive website for an auto repair and body shop called "AQ Prestige Euro Performance". Use the design structure, layout, and services from https://www.nizomotors.com/ as a structural reference, but elevate it significantly in terms of aesthetics, typography, visual quality, and modern feel.

Branding & Vibe:

Company Name: AQ Prestige Euro Performance

Services Offered: Euro Performance Tuning, Auto Repair, and Body Work

Aesthetics: Premium, high-end, sleek, modern, and trustworthy. Dark or modern high-contrast theme with accent colors fitting luxury European automotive branding (e.g., sleek dark gray/black background with bold luxury accents like silver, gold, or vibrant red/blue).

Imagery: Use high-resolution, professional, royalty-free stock photos of European luxury cars (BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Exotic Cars), sleek modern garages, and precision mechanical work.

Key Features & Sections:

Hero Section: Full-width high-impact imagery or background video feel, strong modern typography, clear call-to-action (CTA) buttons like "Book an Appointment" and "Get a Free Body Work Quote."

Services Showcase: Grid/card layout with clean icons and hover effects highlighting services (Diagnostics, Engine Repair, Euro Performance Tuning, Body Shop & Paint, Brake & Suspension, Scheduled Maintenance).

About Us / Why Choose Us: Highlighting expertise in European vehicles, certified technicians, and state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment.

Gallery / Portfolio: Interactive photo gallery showcasing past body work, repairs, and performance builds with smooth transitions/lightbox.

Testimonials / Reviews: Modern carousel or clean grid displaying customer reviews.

Contact & Appointment Booking: Integrated contact form, address map, phone number, working hours, and easy online appointment/quote request.

Footer: Quick links, social media icons, copyright, and location info.

Technical & UX Requirements:

100% mobile-friendly and responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Smooth scrolling, sleek micro-interactions, clean hover animations, and fast load performance.

Intuitive navigation menu (sticky header on scroll).
